# Cottage Cheese Recipes Plan (Diabetic-Friendly)

Backlog of popular cottage cheese recipes that fit the `diabetic-friendly` tag. Cottage cheese is high in protein (~14g per 1/2 cup) and low in carbs (~4-5g), but runs 350-450mg sodium per 1/2 cup, so recipes should call out low-sodium options.
